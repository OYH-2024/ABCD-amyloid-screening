Welcome to the Amyloid Binding Compounds Database

Based on multi-target screening results, a database of small molecules that inhibit amyloid aggregation has been constructed. This database not only provides extensive information on relevant small molecules but also integrates online docking, online molecular descriptor computation, and online prediction of small molecule activity. It serves as an important reference for further analysis of the mechanisms by which small molecules inhibit amyloid aggregation and for subsequent screening efforts.
